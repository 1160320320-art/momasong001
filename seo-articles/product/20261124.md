# Kids Swimwear OEM Quotations: What to Include in Your Brief

A useful kids swimwear OEM quotation starts with a clear brief. The brief does not have to be a finished tech pack, but it should tell the factory what you want to make, in which sizes and colors, and where the order will go. MOMASONG is a kids swimwear factory in Dongguan with 15+ years' experience. This page explains what to include so a quotation can be prepared against confirmed terms — not a generic form letter.

This article is about the quotation brief. It is not a full MOQ explainer or a sample-to-bulk checklist. Those points appear here only where they affect how you write the request.

## Start with the two order options

MOMASONG quotes two quantity options, and they should not be collapsed into one headline number:

- Usual MOQ: 300 pcs per style per color.
- Small batch: from 50 pcs per style per color.

Colors cannot be combined to meet the MOQ. Sizes may be mixed within the same style and color. If you plan two colors of the same style, treat each color as its own quantity. If you plan several sizes of one color, you can show a size breakdown in the brief.

Sample fees are quoted case by case, depending on style, fabric and sizes. A sample quote includes one revision, and the fee is credited against payment when the bulk order is placed. This article does not state a sample-day number; timing for samples is confirmed when the sample is quoted.

Bulk lead time depends on quantity and is usually 15–20 days. Confirm that timing in the quotation; do not treat the range as a guarantee before the quote is issued.

## What to include in the brief

### Style references

Share what you already have: photos, sketches, or a tech pack. If you only have a reference idea — for example, a long-sleeve rash guard set for a stated size range — you can still send an inquiry and discuss development. MOMASONG does not treat an inquiry as a promise of free design work. The brief should simply make the starting point clear.

### Size range and size breakdown

Name the size range you intend to buy and, if you can, the quantity in each size. MOMASONG sizes are labeled in the 2T–14T style. A size breakdown helps the quotation reflect a mixed-size run within one style and color. It is not a recommended ratio and it is not a substitute for checking the [size guide](https://momasong.com/size-guide).

If measurements or a buyer size chart will be used, attach them. If you will use the MOMASONG chart, say so.

### Colorways

List the colors or attach artwork, color references or print files when you have them. Each color is quoted against its own MOQ. If a colorway is still open, say what is decided and what is still to be confirmed.

### Fabric, labels and packaging — only if you have a request

Include a fabric target only if you already have one. This article does not list standard fabric names, weights or performance claims, and a quotation should not assume them. If you need brand labels, hangtags or specific packaging, describe those requirements so they can be included in the quote. If you do not specify them, they can be discussed after the first reply; this page does not describe a default packing method.

### Test reports and certificates

Test reports are available. If you need certificates, ask so that request can be confirmed in the quotation. Certificates are provided only when the buyer asks and only after they are confirmed. This article does not name certificates, and it does not make a UPF claim.

### Destination and trade terms

Say the destination country or port, and whether you want the quote on FOB Shenzhen terms or want a DDP discussion. DDP totals are calculated per order and confirmed before quoting. Share an estimated quantity per style and color, and any timing you are working toward. Timing is confirmed in the quotation.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can colors be combined to meet the MOQ?

No. Colors cannot be combined. Each color of a style is quoted on its own. Sizes may be mixed within the same style and color.

### What is the usual quantity and the small-batch option?

The usual MOQ is 300 pcs per style per color. A small batch starts from 50 pcs per style per color. Say which option you are asking about, or ask for both to be compared in the quote.

### How are sample fees handled?

The sample fee is quoted case by case. It includes one revision and is credited against payment when the bulk order is placed. Sample timing is confirmed with the sample quote, not as a standard number on this page.

### How long does bulk production take?

Bulk lead time depends on quantity and is usually 15–20 days. Confirm the figure in the quotation.

### Can I still inquire if I do not have a tech pack?

Yes. Send the references you have and the size, color and destination details you already know. Development can be discussed from that starting point.
