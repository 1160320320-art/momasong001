# Custom Swimwear Manufacturers: What to Confirm Before You Order

Before you place a kids swimwear order, it helps to confirm the brief, quantity options, timing and documentation with the custom swimwear manufacturers you are considering. MOMASONG is a kids swimwear factory in Dongguan, China, with 15+ years of experience. Buyers comparing custom swimwear manufacturers usa listings should note that this factory is in Dongguan, not in the United States.

This page is a confirmation list for a custom kids swimwear order. It is not a full MOQ explainer, a sample-to-bulk checklist, a quotation-brief template, a shipping guide, or a label-and-packaging checklist.

## What to confirm with custom swimwear manufacturers

### The brief

Write down the styles you want, the size run, the colors, and any artwork or reference images. If you need your brand on the garment or the packing, include label, tag, hangtag and packaging requirements in the same brief. Those details should be confirmed on the quotation. This article does not assume a default label type or attachment method.

For custom production context, see the [OEM/ODM page](https://momasong.com/oem-odm). You can also browse the [shop](https://momasong.com/shop).

### The kids size run

Not every style is graded in every size. Sizes may be mixed within the same style and color, and that size mix should be confirmed when the order is placed and shown on the quotation. See the [size guide](https://momasong.com/size-guide) for the published kids size range, then confirm the size run for each style on the quotation.

### Quantity options, including a low MOQ choice

Some manufacturer pages advertise a low minimum. A custom swimwear manufacturer low moq option is available here as a distinct choice, not as a way to pool colors or styles together:

- Usual MOQ: 300 pcs per style per color.
- Small batch: from 50 pcs per style per color.

These are two options, not one combined pool of pieces. Colors cannot be combined to meet the MOQ. Sizes may be mixed within the same style and color.

### Lead time and trade terms

Bulk lead time depends on quantity and is usually 15–20 days. The schedule for a specific order is confirmed with the quotation. Do not treat the 15–20 day range as a door-to-door delivery time.

FOB Shenzhen is available. DDP is available; totals are calculated per order and confirmed before they are quoted. A DDP figure should not be assumed from a previous order or from an FOB price alone.

Sample fees are quoted case by case, depending on style, fabric and sizes. A sample quote includes one revision, and the fee is credited against payment when the bulk order is placed. This article does not state a sample-day number.

### Test reports and certificates

Test reports can be provided. Certificates are confirmed only if the buyer asks; this article does not list certificate names.

This article does not make a UPF 50+ claim for a custom program as a whole. Any UPF wording should have a confirmed basis and should be agreed in the quotation.
