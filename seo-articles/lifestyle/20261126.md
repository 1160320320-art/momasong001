# Kids Swimwear OEM Lead Times and Shipping: FOB Shenzhen or DDP

A kids swimwear OEM quotation should state how long bulk production is expected to take and on which trade terms the price is based. MOMASONG is a kids swimwear factory in Dongguan with 15+ years' experience. This page explains the usual bulk lead time and the two shipping options that are confirmed in a quotation: FOB Shenzhen, or DDP calculated per order.

This article is about lead time and shipping. It is not a full MOQ explainer, a sample-to-bulk checklist, or a guide to writing a quotation brief. Those points appear here only where they affect timing or the terms of the quote.

## Usual bulk lead time

Bulk lead time depends on quantity and is usually 15–20 days. The schedule for a specific order is confirmed with the quotation and order details. Do not treat the 15–20 day range as a guarantee before the quote is issued.

The quantity option you use is part of that confirmation:

- Usual MOQ: 300 pcs per style per color.
- Small batch: from 50 pcs per style per color.

These are two options, not one combined pool of pieces. Colors cannot be combined toward the MOQ. Sizes may be mixed within the same style and color; the size mix should be confirmed when the order is placed. Not every style is graded in every size; confirm the size run in the quotation. See the [size guide](https://momasong.com/size-guide) for the published kids size range.

Sample fees are quoted case by case, depending on style, fabric requirements and sizes. A sample quote includes one revision, and the fee is credited against payment when the bulk order is placed. This article does not state a sample-day number; timing for samples is confirmed when the sample is quoted.

## FOB Shenzhen or DDP

Standard shipping terms are FOB Shenzhen. If you want the quotation on FOB Shenzhen terms, say so in the inquiry, along with the destination country or port where that helps the discussion.

DDP is available. DDP totals need freight and duties calculated per order and confirmed before they are quoted. A DDP figure should not be assumed from a previous order or from an FOB price alone.

The usual 15–20 day range is a bulk production lead time confirmed with the quotation. It should not be read as a door-to-door delivery time. Transit after the goods are ready depends on the agreed trade terms and the booking for that order, and is confirmed in the quotation.
