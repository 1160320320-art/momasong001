# Private Label Swimwear Manufacturers: What to Confirm on Labels and Branding

A private-label kids swimwear range is not finished when the fabric and fit are approved. The goods still have to carry the buyer's brand in a way that was agreed before production: neck labels, care tags, hangtags and packaging. Those details are easy to leave until later. With private label swimwear manufacturers, they should be written into the brief and confirmed on the quotation.

This page is not a general factory-selection guide, and it is not a tour of the production floor. It is a practical checklist of branding points to confirm with MOMASONG, a kids swimwear factory in Dongguan with 15+ years' experience.

## Start with a private-label brief, not an assumption

Private label swimwear is swimwear that carries the buyer's brand. The exact label wording, any logo files, care text and packaging needs should be stated in the brief rather than assumed.

MOMASONG's factory is in Dongguan, China. Buyers sometimes look for a private label swimwear manufacturer in the USA or Australia, or in Miami; this factory is not in those locations.

The live [OEM/ODM page](https://momasong.com/oem-odm) lists customization options that are relevant here: custom woven neck labels, screen-printed care tags, brand hangtags, and custom packaging (the page mentions bags, custom labels and branded shipping boxes). Treat those as options to ask for and confirm on the quotation — not as items that are automatically included, free, or guaranteed in every order.

## What to confirm on labels, tags, hangtags and packaging

### Neck labels

If the order needs a custom woven neck label, put the wording you want — and any logo or language requirements — in the brief. Material, size, placement and artwork format should be confirmed on the quotation, not guessed in advance.

### Care and content tags

The OEM/ODM page lists screen-printed care tags as an option. Supply the care and content wording you need for the markets where the goods will be sold, and confirm the details on the quotation. This article does not provide a standard care sentence, and it does not give legal advice on labeling rules.

### Hangtags

Brand hangtags are listed as an option. If you need them, include the copy and any layout files in the brief so they can be reviewed with the quotation. Do not assume a default size, material or attachment method.

### Packaging

If you need custom packaging, say so in the brief. Confirm the format, branding and quantity on the quotation. This article does not treat packaging as eco-certified or biodegradable by default.

If you need a particular language on a label, tag, hangtag or package, include the exact wording in the brief and confirm it on the quotation.

## Order terms that still need to be clear

Private-label branding sits on top of the same confirmed order terms:

- Usual MOQ: 300 pcs per style per color.
- Small batch: from 50 pcs per style per color.

These are two options, not one headline number. Colors cannot be combined to meet the MOQ. Sizes may be mixed within the same style and color. Any effect of label or packaging work on timing or cost is confirmed on the quotation; this article does not invent a separate minimum for hangtags or bags.

Bulk lead time is usually 15–20 days and depends on quantity. The figure is confirmed on the quotation. It is not a door-to-door delivery time.

Sample fees are quoted case by case, depending on style, fabric and sizes. A sample quote includes one revision, and the fee is credited against payment when the bulk order is placed. This article does not state a sample-day number. If a sample also needs to show a label or tag, say that when the sample is quoted.

FOB Shenzhen is available. DDP is available; totals are calculated per order and confirmed before quoting.

## Test reports and certificates

Test reports can be provided. Certificates are confirmed only if the buyer asks; this article does not list certificate names. This article does not make a UPF 50+ claim for a private-label program as a whole. Any UPF wording should have a confirmed basis and should be agreed in the quotation.
