## Why children's swimwear needs to be more than a swimsuit

Children's skin is thinner and more sensitive than adult skin, which means it burns faster and needs more protection during long days outdoors. A regular swimsuit only covers a small part of the body, leaving shoulders, backs, arms, and legs exposed to direct UV rays. That is where UPF-rated swimwear changes the game.

UPF, or Ultraviolet Protection Factor, measures how much ultraviolet radiation a fabric blocks. A UPF 50+ fabric blocks more than 98% of UV rays, giving children a consistent layer of sun defense that cannot be washed away, rubbed off, or forgotten halfway through the day the way sunscreen often is. It does not replace sunscreen on exposed skin, but it dramatically reduces the amount of skin that needs it.

Sun protection is only half of the story, though. A good kids' swimsuit also has to feel good. Scratchy tags, stiff seams, and fabric that stays cold and heavy after leaving the water are quick ways to turn a happy beach day into an early departure.

## What makes a swimsuit truly sun-safe

When comparing kids' swimwear, these are the features that matter most:

### UPF 50+ rated fabric

Look for the UPF rating on the label or product page. MOMASONG styles are made with UPF 50+ fabric designed for extended water play. The protection is built into the textile, so it stays effective after repeated swimming and washing.

### More coverage where the sun hits hardest

Shoulders, upper back, and the back of the neck are some of the first places children burn. Rash guards, long-sleeve sets, and one-piece suits with higher backs cover those sensitive areas better than traditional bikinis or trunk-only styles. For younger children, a one-piece rash guard suit is one of the simplest ways to get head-to-toe coverage.

### Soft, comfortable construction

Flat seams, stretchy openings, and soft elastic waistbands prevent chafing and make getting dressed easier. When a child can pull on a swimsuit without a fight, everyone wins.

## Finding the right size

Kids' swimwear sizes can be tricky because children grow at different rates and swimwear is meant to fit more snugly than everyday clothes. MOMASONG covers sizes from 2T through 16T, with most styles offering a full size run so families can find the right fit for toddlers, young children, and preteens.

A few practical fit tips:

- Rash guard tops should fit snugly but not tightly, with enough room to move freely.
- Waistbands should sit comfortably without digging in or sliding down.
- If a child is between sizes, size up for more growing room, especially for a full summer season.
- Check the size chart for each style, because one-piece suits, two-piece sets, and swim dresses can fit differently.

## How to care for kids' swimwear

Good care keeps swimwear looking bright and performing well all season long:

- Rinse suits in cold, fresh water after every swim to remove chlorine, salt, and sand.
- Hand wash or use a gentle machine cycle with mild detergent.
- Avoid bleach, fabric softeners, and high-heat drying, which can break down stretch fibers.
- Lay flat or hang to dry in the shade instead of using a hot dryer.
- Rotate between two or three suits if the child swims every day, so each one has time to recover its shape.
