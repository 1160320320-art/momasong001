Buying guide

Kids Swimwear OEM MOQ: 300 per Style per Color, or a 50-Piece Small Batch

MOMASONG November 22, 2026

Buyers often see a single “low MOQ” number on factory websites. MOMASONG uses two different options for kids swimwear OEM and wholesale orders. Confirm which option applies when you enquire; do not add quantities across colors to meet a minimum.

Two MOQ options, not one headline number

  • Usual MOQ: 300 pcs per style per color.
  • Small batch: from 50 pcs per style per color.

These are two options, not one combined pool of pieces. A small-batch order is not automatically counted toward a 300-piece run, and a 300-piece order is not simply a “larger version” of a 50-piece order without confirming the terms. Which option you should use depends on the order and is confirmed in the quotation.

For the custom process from brief to bulk, see OEM/ODM Custom Swimwear. To request a quote, use the contact page.

Color breaks and size mixing

Colors cannot be combined toward the MOQ. If you need two colors of the same style, each color has its own minimum under the option you are using. For example, under the usual 300-piece option, 300 pcs in one color plus 300 pcs in another color are two colorways, each meeting the minimum; 200 pcs in one color plus 100 pcs in another color does not meet 300 pcs per color.

Sizes can be mixed within the same style and color. The size mix should be confirmed when the order is placed. See the size guide for the published kids size range. Not every style is graded in every size; confirm the size run in the quotation.

Lead time, samples and test reports

Bulk lead time depends on quantity and is usually 15–20 days. The schedule for a specific order is confirmed with the quotation and order details.

Sample fees are quoted case by case, based on the style, fabric requirements and sizes. The sample fee includes one revision and is credited against payment when the bulk order is placed.

Test reports are available for all products.

Quality process pages are at quality. Current styles can be reviewed in the product shop.

Frequently asked questions

What is the MOQ for kids swimwear OEM?

There are two options: a usual MOQ of 300 pcs per style per color, and a small batch from 50 pcs per style per color. Confirm which option applies in the quotation.

Can colors be combined to meet the MOQ?

No. Colors cannot be combined toward the MOQ. Each color of a style is counted on its own.

Can sizes be mixed?

Yes. Sizes can be mixed within the same style and color. Confirm the size breakdown when you place the order.

How long does bulk production take?

Bulk lead time depends on quantity and is usually 15–20 days. The timing for your order is confirmed with the order details.

Do you provide samples?

Yes. Sample fees are quoted case by case (style, fabric requirements and sizes), include one revision, and are credited against payment when the bulk order is placed.

Do you provide test reports?

Yes. Test reports are available for all products.
